# 📌 About AgriFlow

Agricultural procurement can often involve long waiting times, manual paperwork, unclear queue positions, and difficulty tracking procurement and payment status.

A farmer may have to visit a procurement centre, wait for their turn, provide produce details, go through quality and weighing procedures, and then wait for the procurement and payment process to be completed. When these steps are handled manually, it can become difficult for both farmers and procurement-centre staff to keep track of everything.

**AgriFlow** is designed to bring these steps together into one digital platform.

Our goal is simple:

> **Give farmers better visibility and control over their procurement journey while helping procurement centres manage their operations digitally.**

With AgriFlow, a farmer can register their details and produce, book a suitable procurement slot, receive a digital token, track their queue position, view procurement information, and receive updates about their payment.

At the procurement-centre side, staff can manage farmer information, produce details, quality and weighing records, procurement information, and payment records.

# 🎯 The Problem We Are Addressing

The traditional procurement process can create several practical difficulties for farmers and procurement centres.

### For Farmers

- Uncertain waiting times at procurement centres
- No clear information about their position in the queue
- Manual registration and paperwork
- Difficulty tracking procurement progress
- Limited visibility of price and transaction details
- Uncertainty about payment status
- Language barriers when using digital systems

### For Procurement Centres

- Large amounts of manual data entry
- Paper-based records
- Difficulty managing queues
- Repetitive record keeping
- Difficulty maintaining procurement information
- Additional effort required to communicate updates to farmers

These challenges create a need for a system that can make the procurement process more organized and transparent.

# 💡 Our Solution

AgriFlow provides a **single digital workflow** that connects farmers with procurement centres.

Instead of treating registration, queue management, procurement, and payment as separate processes, our platform brings them together.
